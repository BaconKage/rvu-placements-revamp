How a student gets there

Five gates, in order.

01

Register with CAR

Registration & Declaration Form filed with Corporate & Alumni Relations. Nothing proceeds without it.

02

Pre-placement training

Domain and technical training, soft skills, behavioural and networking intelligence. 80% attendance is mandatory.

03

Clear eligibility

No academic backlogs. No pending disciplinary cases. Internship or capstone complete.

04

Sit the drives

250+ organisations run on campus. Apply only to roles you would genuinely join.

05

Honour the offer

An accepted offer is final. Concerns go to CAR, not to the recruiter.
